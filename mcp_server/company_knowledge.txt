Vellora Therapeutics Manufacturing Guidelines

Manufacturing batches must be stored between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius.

Quality inspection is mandatory before shipment.

If contamination is detected, the entire batch must be quarantined immediately.

Employees must report equipment failures within one hour.

Product recalls require approval from the Quality Assurance Manager.

Production orders cannot begin without supplier verification.

Medicine storage rooms are inspected every Monday.

Expired medicines must be destroyed following company policy.

Every manufacturing batch receives a unique batch number.

Quality tests include purity, stability, and contamination analysis.
